description: "The benchmark is built to take more questions — `questions.json` is the whole interface and `bench init` already rewrites it — but nothing on the published site says so. A reader who thinks of a good question has no visible way to offer it. The repository side is largely done already (the `add_question.yml` and `add_model_or_provider.yml` issue forms, CONTRIBUTING sections naming the one file to change, a PR template); what is missing is the invitation and the route to it.\n\nAdd a contribution block to the site offering two routes, because they suit different people. **Open a pull request** (or the templated issue form) for someone comfortable editing `questions.json`, and **send it to En Dash** for someone who just has an idea, via the contact modal at endash.us:\n\n    https://endash.us/?showContact=true\n      &contactTitle=Submit%20a%20question%20to%20the%20Hotdog%20Benchmark\n      &contactMessage=<prefill, see below>\n      &contactSource=hotdogbenchmark-lol\n\n`contactTitle` renders as the modal's heading in HTML, so it is human-readable prose with encoded spaces, not a slug. The model route gets its own title (\"Suggest a model for the Hotdog Benchmark\"). Both are built in one place from the site's own name so a fork does not advertise this repository's.\n\nThe `contactMessage` prefill carries the fields the registry needs (the question ending in \"One word answer.\", the subject as a noun phrase, why it is worth asking), asks how the submitter wants to be credited, and — this is the part that must not be dropped — tells them **En Dash will email them when the question goes live**, so they should give an email address or write to hello@endash.us. The PR and issue templates say the same thing, since a contributor who opens a PR under a handle with no public email otherwise never hears back.\n\nExplicitly deferred: accounts, sign-in, and bring-your-own-key self-service submission. That arc lives in the \"Run your own benchmark\" epic. This is the MVP that works with no backend and no auth.\n\nOpen decision for whoever picks this up: whether the GitHub CTA points at a prefilled new-file pull request or at the existing `add_question.yml` issue form. The issue form already exists and is tested, and it is far lower friction for a non-coder; the PR route is better for someone who would rather just edit the JSON. Offering both is acceptable."
