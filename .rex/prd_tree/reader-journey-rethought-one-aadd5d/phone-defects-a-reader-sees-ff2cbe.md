description: "Four things visible on the live site at phone width, plus the reason the gates did not catch the worst of them.\n\n**The answer word is clipped.** On the answer board at 375 and 390 px, any row with the REASONING tag shows \"N…\" or \"Y…\" instead of its word. `.word` in AnswerBoard.astro sets `white-space: nowrap` and `text-overflow: ellipsis`, and the tag takes the space. The project's own `.responsive/home@375.png` shows it. Fix: the word never truncates. Let it wrap or shrink, move the tag under the word on narrow rows, and drop the ellipsis rule entirely; an ellipsis on the one word the site exists to show is never right.\n\n**The gate gap behind it.** `scripts/responsive.mjs` fails on horizontal overflow, target size, obscured focus and text spacing, but a `text-overflow: ellipsis` element hides its overflow from all of those. Add a truncation check: for every element whose computed `text-overflow` is `ellipsis`, or whose `scrollWidth` exceeds `clientWidth` with `overflow: hidden`, fail unless it is inside an intentional scroll region. Verify the check by breaking the board on purpose first.\n\n**The repeated-date sentence.** The history page says \"Week 36, 2026 was run 4 times, on September 2, 2026, September 2, 2026, September 2, 2026, September 2, 2026.\" Collapse same-day runs to one date with a count, or show times when the dates match.\n\n**The twins line.** \"Thinking alike: Claude Opus 5 & GPT-5.4 mini · … · and 22 more pairs.\" With three binary questions, 25 of 55 pairs agree on everything, so the line advertises that the statistic does not discriminate. Render it only when the registry has at least five questions, or when the agreeing pairs are fewer than a quarter of all pairs; otherwise nothing.\n\n**Nav scroll affordance.** Under 52 rem the nav is a scrolling pill row with the scrollbar hidden; at 390 px \"HOW IT WORKS\" and \"ABOUT\" are cut at the right edge with nothing suggesting there is more. Add an edge fade (a mask or gradient overlay that respects forced colors) and let the last visible pill peek. No hamburger, no JavaScript."
